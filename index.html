My dear Princess Gudu,

Princess, I’m so sorry for the hurt I caused you. Thinking about the pain I brought you fills me with regret. You deserved care, honesty, and kindness from me, and I’m deeply ashamed that my actions fell short of that. I take responsibility for the pain I brought into your life. There is no excuse that makes it okay.

It hurts to know that I made someone I care about so deeply feel hurt, unheard, or unimportant. Princess, I wish I had been more thoughtful with your heart. Your feelings matter, and I wish I had shown you that more clearly. I can’t undo what happened, but I can listen without defending myself, acknowledge where I went wrong, and do the work to change.

Princess Gudu, thank you for being willing to hear me. It means so much to have the chance to speak honestly to you. I don’t take your yes as a promise that everything is healed, or that you owe me forgiveness. You deserve time, space, and the freedom to decide what feels right for you. I will respect that.

If we try again, I want to earn your trust through patience, consistency, and actions that match my words. You matter to me, Princess, and I want you to feel that in the way I treat you every day. I’m truly sorry for letting you down. More than asking you to take me back, I want to become someone who treats your heart with the care it has always deserved.

for you, Princess with love and remorse ♡
made with a hopeful heart ♡
